# Evidence-led store review

Apply store-context. Read `playbooks/principles.md` and the store's brand, operations, and aggregated reports. Use only data and accounts authorized for this store.

State the reporting period, source, currency, timezone, and data limitations. Evaluate contribution profit, conversion, acquisition efficiency, average order value, retention, refunds, fulfilment, and cash where evidence exists. Separate measurement from estimates and correlation from causal claims.

Review the customer journey and app stack against a specific problem. Rank a small number of actions by expected impact, confidence, effort, cost, and reversibility. Do not invent benchmarks, customer feedback, or attribution certainty. Save a store-specific recommendation and measurable next experiment; do not make live changes as part of a review without authorization.
