# Shopify operating principles

1. Protect contribution profit and cash. Evaluate revenue alongside gross margin, acquisition cost, discounts, fulfilment, returns, and working capital. Label assumptions and uncertain attribution.
2. Start with a specific customer problem and a clear product promise. Keep positioning and evidence in each store's brand directory.
3. Keep product information accurate: variants, prices, stock, shipping expectations, materials, care, claims, and return terms. Do not invent reviews, scarcity, certifications, or performance claims.
4. Make the buying journey fast, accessible, and understandable on mobile. Measure meaningful conversion and retention outcomes before adding apps or interface complexity.
5. Treat changes as hypotheses. Record the baseline, expected outcome, observation window, and rollback. Avoid calling a small or biased sample a proven result.
6. Use the smallest useful app stack. Review app permissions, recurring costs, data access, page performance, and overlapping functionality.
7. Protect customer trust: consent, privacy, clear offers, responsive support, and accurate delivery promises.
8. Share methods across stores; keep store identities and private information separate. Every live operation must target a verified store account.
9. Prefer reversible changes and staged releases. Publishing and spending require the user's authorization for the specific action.
10. Preserve a usable history. Commit decisions and reproducible work; keep credentials and personal customer information in appropriate external systems.
